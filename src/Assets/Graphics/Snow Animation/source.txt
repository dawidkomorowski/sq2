Description:

    Tileable snow animations that I created for Stendhal. I planned to eventually upload them & figured that now would be the perfect time with the current Ice is Nice art challenge.

Details:

    Orientation: 2d/orthogonal
    Dimensions: 64x64
    Animation:
        3 variants: light, medium, & heavy
        16 frames
        preview @ 100ms delay
    PNG images optimized with indexed color (smaller files)
